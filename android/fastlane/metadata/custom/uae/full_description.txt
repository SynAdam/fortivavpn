SAFER ON PUBLIC WI-FI
Wi-Fi in malls, airports, hotels and cafes is shared with people you do not know. Fortiva encrypts your traffic between your phone and our servers, so other people on the same network cannot read what you send and receive. It works the same on mobile data.

HOW FORTIVA WORKS
Fortiva VPN uses Android's VpnService to create a secure, encrypted tunnel between your device and our remote servers. All network traffic is encrypted with TLS, so your data stays private and protected.
• Creates a secure device-level VPN tunnel
• Encrypts all data between your device and VPN servers using TLS
• Does not collect, share, or redirect user traffic for monetization
• Does not manipulate traffic from other apps
• Full privacy policy at fortivavpn.com/privacy
