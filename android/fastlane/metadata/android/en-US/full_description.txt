HOW FORTIVA WORKS
FortivaVPN uses Android's VpnService to create a secure, encrypted tunnel between your device and our remote servers. All network traffic is encrypted with TLS, ensuring your data remains private and protected.
• Creates a secure device-level VPN tunnel
• Encrypts all data between your device and VPN servers using TLS
• Does not collect, share, or redirect user traffic for monetization
• Does not manipulate traffic from other apps
• Full privacy policy: fortivavpn.com/privacy
