# Security

If you find a security problem in the apps, please tell us privately rather
than in an issue: a way to read another user's traffic, account or
credentials, to take over the app's connection, to learn a user's real
address, or anything that would put the people who use the app at risk.

- **On GitHub:** the repository's **Security** tab, then **Report a
  vulnerability**. Only we can read the report.
- **By email:** admin@fortivavpn.com, with "Security" in the subject.

Say what is affected, the app version, and how to reproduce it. We answer
within a week, and tell you what we will do and when. Please give us time
to ship a fix before you publish anything.

Out of scope: that a network or a government blocks the service, and
reports from automated scanners with nothing shown to be exploitable.

[The README](README.md) is in every language of the app.
