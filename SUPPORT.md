# Getting help

- **Help with the app, your account or a payment:** open Settings in the app
  and tap Contact Us. Messages there are not public.
- **News and new versions:** [fortivavpn.com](https://fortivavpn.com) and
  the Telegram channels, in [English](https://t.me/fortivavpn), [Arabic](https://t.me/fortivavpn_ar), [Chinese](https://t.me/fortivavpn_zh), [Hindi](https://t.me/fortivavpn_hi), [Indonesian](https://t.me/fortivavpn_id), [Persian](https://t.me/fortiva_vpn), [Portuguese](https://t.me/fortivavpn_pt), [Russian](https://t.me/fortivavpn_ru), [Spanish](https://t.me/fortivavpn_es), [Turkish](https://t.me/fortivavpn_tr), [Ukrainian](https://t.me/fortivavpn_ua), [Urdu](https://t.me/fortivavpn_ur), [Uzbek](https://t.me/fortivavpn_uz) and [Vietnamese](https://t.me/fortivavpn_vi).
- **A bug or an idea:** [open an issue](https://github.com/SynAdam/fortivavpn/issues/new/choose)
  with one of the forms. English is best if you can, and any other language
  is fine too.
- **A security problem:** do not open an issue. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  says how to tell us privately.

Issues are public, so anyone can read them. Never post a link from Copy
link, your Premium key, a code from Help a friend connect, or a screenshot
that shows one of them. Anyone who has one can use it.

[The README](README.md) is in every language of the app.
