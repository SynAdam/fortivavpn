# Getting help

- **Help with the app, your account or a payment:** open Settings in the app
  and tap Contact Us. Messages there are not public.
- **News and new versions:** the Telegram channels, in English at
  [t.me/fortivavpn](https://t.me/fortivavpn) and in Persian at
  [t.me/fortiva_vpn](https://t.me/fortiva_vpn), and
  [fortivavpn.com](https://fortivavpn.com).
- **A bug or an idea:** [open an issue](https://github.com/SynAdam/fortivavpn/issues/new/choose)
  with one of the forms. Write in English or Persian.
- **A security problem:** do not open an issue. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  says how to tell us privately.

Issues are public, so anyone can read them. Never post a link from Copy
link, your Premium key, a code from Help a friend connect, or a screenshot
that shows one of them. Anyone who has one can use it.
